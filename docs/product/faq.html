Frequently asked questions

Why didn't my change apply to every page?

Pages with their own override keep their value. A tenant default or a template only reaches pages that don't set the value themselves. Ask the assistant to explain the setting to see which level applies.

The assistant says I don't have permission

Viewers can only read. Ask an editor or an admin to make the change, or ask your account owner to change your role.

Is my data visible to other organisations?

No. Every request is pinned to your own tenant. The assistant has no way to address another tenant's pages, and requests that mention another tenant are refused.

How fast do changes show up?

Changes apply immediately. Explanations and previews from the knowledge graph catch up within a few seconds.
